# Itishree Weds Bikram

A responsive Odia wedding invitation shaped around Bhubaneswar: temple stone, old city lanes, and the energy of a modern capital. Its visual language pairs deep kumkum maroon, warm invitation-paper ivory, antique gold detailing, and the couple's I/B monogram.

## Site files

- `index.html` — invitation, family details, celebrations, travel links, RSVP, and compact Bhubaneswar city notes.
- `assets/styles.css` — site-wide visual system and responsive layouts.
- `assets/palettes.css` — the final ivory, kumkum-maroon, and antique-gold wedding palette, plus the drifting petal layer.
- `assets/script.js` — mobile navigation, countdown, landmark carousel, music toggle, and page-top control.
- `assets/itishree-bikram-hero-ghibli.jpg` — Ghibli-style hero illustration of the couple in the blush-pink lehenga and mint sherwani from their reference photo.
- `assets/itishree-bikram-hero.png` — the earlier hand-painted hero artwork, kept as an alternate.
- `assets/lingaraj-bindu.jpg`, `assets/mukteswar.jpg`, `assets/dhauli.jpg`, `assets/udayagiri-khandagiri.jpg`, `assets/kalinga-stadium.jpg`, `assets/rajarani.jpg`, `assets/ananta-vasudeva.jpg`, `assets/kalabhoomi.jpg`, and `assets/nandankanan.jpg` — the nine watercolour scenes in the Bhubaneswar landmark carousel.
- `assets/ib-monogram.png`, `assets/favicon.png`, and `assets/apple-touch-icon.png` — the couple’s rose-gold I/B laurel monogram for the header, RSVP seal, footer, and browser icons.
- `assets/temple-watermark.svg` — decorative Bhubaneswar inspired line art.

## Celebrations

- Mehendi — Saturday, 12 December 2026, 6:00 PM onwards, at the bride's residence.
- Haldi — Sunday, 13 December 2026, 8:00 AM, at the bride's residence.
- Wedding — Sunday, 13 December 2026, 7:30 PM onwards, at The Regal Palm.

The city carousel includes nine illustrated notes covering eleven familiar Bhubaneswar landmarks; use its arrows or dots to explore them.

The site uses one final stationery-inspired palette throughout: warm ivory, deep kumkum maroon, and antique-gold accents.

The YouTube wedding song starts muted when the page loads. Tap the speaker button to turn sound on; browsers may block autoplay with sound until a visitor interacts with the page.

## GitHub Pages

This is a static site. Publish `main` from the repository root in **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment**. Keep `index.html` and the complete `assets` folder at the root level.
